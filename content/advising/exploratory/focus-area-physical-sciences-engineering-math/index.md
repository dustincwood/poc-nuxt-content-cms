        You may be interested in the Physical Sciences, Engineering, & Technology focus area if you value intellectual effort, scientific inquiry, and math. You may have a desire to understand and predict physical and biological phenomena and practices. You may like to work with theories, do research, or design and test ideas and systems. You may be apt to be analytical, cautious, independent, curious, introspective, critical, studious.<br>

Engineering involves a large component of design and working with computers and technology. <br>
