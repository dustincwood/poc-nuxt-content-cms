      #text
      Not sure what to major in? You’re not alone—and you’re in the right place. Exploratory Advising at Utah State University is here to support students who are still deciding on a major or considering a change. Whether you have a few thoughts or no clue where to start, we’re here to help you discover your strengths, explore your interests, and connect you to a meaningful academic path.

    ## Deciding your major can feel like a big decision

    ::::

    ::::moca-text
    ---
    text_style: lead
    ---
    #text
    Here are some steps to help you get started.

          title: Discover More About You!
          title_color: primary
          bar_color: primary
          ---
          #body
          Not sure where to start? Exploratory Advising is here to help you identify your strengths, values, and goals.

          - Take the [Focus2 assessment](https://www.usu.edu/career-design-center/students/focus2) and meet with a [Career Design Center](https://usu.joinhandshake.com/appointments/new) or [Academic Advisor](https://studentappts.usu.edu/ramonline/BookAppt?C=C&T=P&P=834) to discuss the results.
          - Enroll in [USU 1400: Exploring Majors and Careers](https://www.usu.edu/career-design-center/students/courses/careerseriescourses) to connect your interests with real-world opportunities.

          #body
          Tools to learn about your options:

          - [Degree Finder](https://www.usu.edu/degrees/) – Search and compare all USU majors and careers.
          - [Focus Areas](/advising/exploratory/majors-courses) – Narrow your interests by common career paths.
          - [Exploration Station Canvas Course](https://usu.instructure.com/enroll/KMKL4G) – Find programs aligned with your goals.
          - Use the [Gathering Information Worksheet](https://usu.box.com/s/930gjxmdfu3475du2af0onej6bz3eqab) to guide conversations with advisors and faculty.
          - Use a [decision-making matrix](https://www.usu.edu/advising/files/decision-making-matrix.pdf) to compare and evaluate options.

          Use these to create a list of questions you can ask your Exploratory Advisor!

          - Build a timeline with specific short- and long-term steps.
          - Understand deadlines and meet with [your desired major's advisor](https://www.usu.edu/advisors/).
          - Be flexible and ready to revise as needed.
          - Talk through your ideas with your Exploratory Advisor.
