        ## Focus Areas, Exploratory Tracks and Common Majors and Courses

        ::::::

        ::::::moca-text
        ---
        text_style: lead
        ---
        #text
        A focus area is a starting point for your academic exploration. These distinct collections of programs and courses allow you to investigate your interests in order to guide you to the major that aligns most closely with your future goals. You are assigned an advisor within your focus area who can help you explore majors and courses at USU. <br>
        <br>
        You can click on each of these broad focus areas to discover the tracks you can explore. Some tracks and majors are considered to be within multiple focus areas.<br>
